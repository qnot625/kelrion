# Business Model and Go-to-Market

## Commercial structure

1. **Core platform subscription** — tenant, organization, identity, workflows, documents, audit, analytics, integrations and administration.
2. **Solution-pack subscriptions** — Branch Flow, Workforce, Internal Operations, Talent, Resources/Finance, Customer Operations and Governance/Intelligence.
3. **Variable usage** — messaging, voice, video, AI, identity checks, storage and payment-provider costs.
4. **Implementation services** — discovery, migration, configuration, integrations, training and rollout.
5. **Enterprise options** — dedicated isolation/region, custom SLA, advanced SSO, audit support and controlled white-labeling.
6. **Marketplace/partner revenue** — connectors, templates, localized packs and managed services.

## Land-and-expand approach

Sell one measurable outcome first:

- Reduce branch waiting and failed visits.
- Stop manual attendance corrections.
- Cut approval and internal-service cycle time.

After the shared tenant, identity, organization and workflow foundation is installed, expand to adjacent packs.

## Pilot package

A pilot must include:

- Defined locations, users and process scope.
- Baseline measurement before launch.
- Success target and decision date.
- Configuration/migration responsibilities.
- Training and support plan.
- Data protection and security terms.
- Post-pilot conversion options.

## Pilot metrics

### Customer flow

- Average and p90 wait time.
- No-show and abandonment rate.
- Service duration and first-time resolution.
- Failed visit/repeat visit rate.
- Customer satisfaction.

### Workforce

- Attendance correction rate.
- Shift coverage and lateness.
- Leave processing time.
- Manager approval delay.
- Payroll-export exceptions.

### Internal operations

- Request cycle time.
- SLA breach rate.
- Rework and transfer rate.
- Unresolved backlog age.
- Policy/document acknowledgement.

### Platform/business

- Time to configure tenant.
- Weekly active users.
- Journey completion.
- Support load.
- Pilot conversion.
- Modules adopted and expansion revenue.
- Gross retention.

## Implementation product

Implementation must become repeatable intellectual property:

- Process-discovery templates.
- Data-import mappings and validation.
- Industry configuration packs.
- Role and permission templates.
- Training curricula.
- Launch checklist.
- Outcome dashboard.
- Partner certification.

## Sales qualification

A prospect is ready when:

- The process pain is measurable.
- A budget owner and operational owner exist.
- Existing systems and integration needs are known.
- The prospect can provide pilot users/data and decision timing.
- The requested outcome fits a standard pack with limited configuration.
- Security/privacy procurement expectations can be met in the target release.

Avoid enterprise deals that require replacing core banking/accounting/payroll before the platform has references and capital for that scope.
