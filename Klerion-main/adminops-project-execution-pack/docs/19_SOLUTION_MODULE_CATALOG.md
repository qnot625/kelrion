### 1. Smart Appointment Scheduling

- **Product layer:** Branch & Customer Flow
- **Strategic phase:** MVP
- **Execution wave:** Pilot MVP
- **Complexity:** Medium
- **Primary users:** Customers, reception, branch managers

**What it is.** A self-service booking system for physical visits, remote consultations, inspections, interviews, account-opening sessions, and any service that requires a time slot. It should support individual staff calendars, pooled teams, branches, service duration, buffers, capacity rules, rescheduling, and recurring appointments.

**Why it matters.** It removes phone-and-paper scheduling, reduces unnecessary travel and crowding, gives customers certainty, and gives managers visibility into demand before the day begins. It is immediately useful to banks, clinics, government offices, schools, professional firms, and SMEs.

**Implementation.** Build a configurable service catalogue linked to branches, staff skills, operating hours, holidays, capacity, and calendars. Provide web/PWA, QR, WhatsApp, call-centre, and later USSD booking channels. Add reminders, deposits where relevant, no-show rules, waitlists, and calendar integration. Store every booking as a workflow object so it can trigger forms, identity checks, tasks, notifications, and analytics.

**Platform connections.** Feeds the virtual queue, visitor management, customer record, branch forecasting, case management, and billing modules.

**Execution rule.** Reuse shared tenant, identity, authorization, workflow, notification, document, audit, analytics and integration services; do not create a new silo for this module.

### 2. Virtual Queue and Remote Check-In

- **Product layer:** Branch & Customer Flow
- **Strategic phase:** MVP
- **Execution wave:** Pilot MVP
- **Complexity:** Medium
- **Primary users:** Walk-in customers, reception, service staff

**What it is.** A digital queue that lets a person join remotely, receive a ticket, see estimated waiting time, check in by QR or code, and receive alerts when their turn is close. It should also handle walk-ins, priority categories, transfers, recalls, missed turns, and multiple service stages.

**Why it matters.** This directly addresses the bank-queue problem in the concept. It reduces physical congestion, improves perceived waiting time, helps vulnerable customers, and produces service-level data that paper tickets cannot provide.

**Implementation.** Create a real-time queue engine with branch, service, priority, capacity, and staff-status rules. Use event streaming or reliable real-time messaging so kiosks, displays, staff consoles, and customer phones stay synchronized. Estimate waits from live throughput and historical patterns, but show ranges rather than false precision. Include an offline fallback and manual override for branch staff.

**Platform connections.** Connects to appointments, service routing, digital signage, customer notifications, SLA analytics, and demand forecasting.

**Execution rule.** Reuse shared tenant, identity, authorization, workflow, notification, document, audit, analytics and integration services; do not create a new silo for this module.

### 3. Branch and Service Discovery with Capacity Routing

- **Product layer:** Branch & Customer Flow
- **Strategic phase:** MVP
- **Execution wave:** Commercial V1
- **Complexity:** Medium
- **Primary users:** Customers, call-centre agents, operations managers

**What it is.** A locator and routing engine that recommends the best branch, department, service channel, or staff member based on distance, opening hours, live queue, available skills, accessibility, equipment, and appointment availability.

**Why it matters.** Customers often visit the wrong branch or arrive when a specialist is unavailable. Smart routing improves first-time resolution, balances workloads across locations, and can reduce pressure on high-traffic branches.

**Implementation.** Maintain a branch capability directory and live operational state. Combine maps, availability, queue length, service eligibility, and organizational rules into a recommendation score. Allow the company to prioritize business policies such as nearest branch, shortest wait, preferred language, or VIP service without hiding alternatives from the user.

**Platform connections.** Uses queue, appointment, staff skills, facility status, and customer preference data.

**Execution rule.** Reuse shared tenant, identity, authorization, workflow, notification, document, audit, analytics and integration services; do not create a new silo for this module.

### 4. Pre-Visit Forms and Document Readiness

- **Product layer:** Branch & Customer Flow
- **Strategic phase:** MVP
- **Execution wave:** Pilot MVP
- **Complexity:** Low
- **Primary users:** Customers, compliance teams, service staff

**What it is.** Digital forms and document checklists completed before a visit, including consent, service details, uploaded evidence, signatures, and eligibility questions. The system should show whether the person is ready, incomplete, or requires manual review.

- **Execution wave:** Advanced

### 17. AI-Assisted Structured Interview Studio

- **Product layer:** Talent & Recruitment
- **Strategic phase:** Advanced
- **Execution wave:** Advanced
- **Complexity:** High
- **Primary users:** Recruiters, hiring managers, applicants, compliance teams

**What it is.** A platform for live or asynchronous video/voice interviews where approved questions are asked consistently, follow-up probes can be suggested, transcripts are generated, evidence is mapped to competencies, and human reviewers make the final decision.

**Why it matters.** It can reduce repetitive first-round screening, make interviews more consistent, support distributed hiring, and create reviewable evidence. However, employment AI is legally and ethically sensitive, so trust and governance are part of the product, not optional add-ons.

**Implementation.** Start with structured question delivery, recording, transcription, note assistance, and job-related evidence extraction. Require applicant notice and consent, accommodations, human review, appeal or reconsideration paths, model/version logs, bias testing, and explainable scorecards. Do not infer emotion, personality, honesty, health, or suitability from facial expressions, accent, tone, or body language; avoid fully autonomous rejection. Treat international recruitment use as a high-governance product area under laws such as the EU AI Act and employment-discrimination rules. [7][9][10][11]

**Platform connections.** Works inside the applicant tracking system and uses scheduling, video, assessments, identity, consent, audit, and analytics services.

**Execution rule.** Reuse shared tenant, identity, authorization, workflow, notification, document, audit, analytics and integration services; do not create a new silo for this module.

### 19. Digital Onboarding and Offboarding

- **Product layer:** Workforce Administration
- **Strategic phase:** MVP
- **Execution wave:** Commercial V1
- **Complexity:** Medium
- **Primary users:** New hires, HR, managers, IT, facilities, finance
